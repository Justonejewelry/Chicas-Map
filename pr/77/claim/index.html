How it works

  1. Submit your Garage Sale or Estate Sale for free.
  2. Pay the one-time $5 boost through Square. Put your sale street address in the payment note.
  3. Chica matches the payment to your submission and reviews the listing. The gold pin is activated after both are confirmed.

Good to know

How long does it last?
For the sale weekend covered by your payment, through Sunday.

Does paying publish my sale?
No. Every listing is reviewed first. Payment alone does not publish a listing or activate its pin.

Will this guarantee more shoppers?
No promotion can guarantee traffic or sales. The gold pin simply makes your approved sale more noticeable on the map.

The boost is optional. Your sale must be approved before its gold pin is activated. Hours, inventory, shopper traffic, and results can vary.
