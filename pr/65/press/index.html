About Chicas Map

Who Chicas Map is, and who Chica is, in a short blurb for a credit line or a note about the site.

Chicas Map is a free, veteran-built map of garage, yard, and estate sales in San Antonio and nearby towns. The mascot is Chica, a cape-wearing chihuahua who helps neighbors find the sales worth the drive. The map is free to browse and free to list.

How to use these images

A short guide for using the logo and photos without changing the brand.

You are welcome to use these files to talk about Chicas Map. Keep the cape magenta, do not stretch or recolor the logo, and do not suggest Chica endorses a product she does not. Need a different crop? Ask on the Contact page.

Brand colors

Read from the site style sheet. The cape pink is the main brand color.

These hex values come from the site CSS variables. --chica-pine is the cape pink, not a green.

  • Cape / brand pink#C513AF · --chica-pine
  • Cape mid#E23BC4 · --chica-pine-mid
  • Cape deep#9A0E88 · --chica-pine-deep
  • Dark background#121212 · --chica-bg
  • Light background#F4F0EA
  • Ink (dark theme)#F4F4F4
  • Ink (light theme)#1A1614
