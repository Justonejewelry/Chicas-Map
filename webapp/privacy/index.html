Privacy

What this map keeps

Chicas Map is a free San Antonio garage-sale map. Location is used to show nearby pins and to unlock a driveway note within 200 feet. It is not sold.

On this device

  • Language and theme stay in local storage on your phone.
  • Sale Intel notes stay on this device until a shared host exists. They do not sync.
  • Near Me asks for location only after you tap Use my location.

What leaves the phone

  • Map tiles come from MapTiler and Esri. A tile request includes the map view, not your name.
  • A privacy-friendly page counter (GoatCounter) records the path, not a profile.
  • Listing a sale or a $5 pin sends the form you submit. Payment runs on Square, not on this map.

What we do not do

No ad trackers. No sale of addresses. No private Facebook groups. Public listings only.
