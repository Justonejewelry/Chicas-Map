Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chica Map ("we") handles information when you use the website, map, PWA, and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Email — if you join the Friday list or contact us.
  • Location — only if you explicitly allow "Near me" or enter a ZIP/address. Location is processed in your browser for distance sorting and is not stored on our servers unless you submit a listing.
  • Sale submissions — title, address, dates, hours, categories, summary, and optional contact you provide.
  • Usage analytics — Microsoft Clarity and similar tools may collect anonymized interaction data to improve the product.
  • Device storage — favorites, routes, and preferences may be stored in your browser's localStorage.

2. How we use information

  • To display and moderate sale listings.
  • To send optional Friday briefings if you subscribe.
  • To improve map quality, performance, and features.
  • To respond to support or sponsorship requests.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Listing details you mark as public may appear on the map. We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics, email) that process data on our behalf under standard terms.

4. Retention

Sale data is kept while relevant to active or recent weekends and for historical quality control. You may request removal of a listing you submitted by contacting us.

5. Your choices

  • Decline location permission — the map still works with ZIP/address search.
  • Unsubscribe from email by replying or contacting us.
  • Clear localStorage in your browser to remove saved favorites/routes.

6. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date.
