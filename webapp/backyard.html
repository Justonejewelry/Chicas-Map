🗺️ Go Treasure Hunting🐾 Explore the Backyard

The map is where Chica hunts.
The Backyard is where she tells you what she found.

Chica Map started with a simple idea: finding a good garage sale shouldn't require opening a dozen websites, chasing broken links, or wondering whether the listing is even still current.

So Chica went sniffing. She searches. She follows the trails. She checks the listings. She organizes the useful information. Then she puts the treasure on a map for the Pack.

Why Chica?

Because somebody had to sniff out the good stuff.

Garage-sale information is scattered across many websites and community sources. People shouldn't have to search multiple sites, open dozens of listings, compare duplicates, figure out what's current, hunt for addresses, find the original source, or copy everything into their own route.

Chica's job is to do the tedious sniffing and give people a cleaner starting point.

Humans have jobs. Chica has a nose.

🐶 One Little Chica vs. The Big Dogs

There are some very big companies doing pieces of what Chica is trying to do. They have teams. They have developers. They have marketing departments. They have budgets with more commas than Chica has bones.

Chica has a map, a laptop, a lot of determination, and a Pack. And she's doing her best.

Chica isn't trying to beat the big companies at being big. She's trying to be useful. She's trying to make garage-sale hunting easier for regular people. She's trying to build something local, independent, and community-powered.

🔎 What Happens Behind the Map?

Basically, Chica does the internet sniffing so you can spend more time doing the treasure hunting.

  1. SniffChica searches the web and approved public sources for potential sales.
  2. FollowShe follows links to individual listings and original sources whenever possible.
  3. CheckDates, times, addresses, descriptions, and sale status are checked.
  4. CleanDuplicate listings and irrelevant information are removed.
  5. MapUseful verified information is organized onto the Chica Map.
  6. ShareChica prepares useful daily information for the Pack.
  7. ImproveThe system gets better as new sources, corrections, and community tips arrive.

🐾 This Isn't Just Chica's Project

Every person who shares Chica Map gives this little project a slightly bigger nose.

The project becomes more useful when people participate. The Pack can help by:

  • Sharing Chica Map
  • Sending sales Chica missed
  • Reporting incorrect information
  • Suggesting useful sources
  • Sharing interesting garage-sale discoveries
  • Telling friends about the project
  • Supporting the project if they want to

👃 Did Chica Miss Something?

Chica searches a lot of places, but even the best nose can miss a scent. If you know about a garage sale, yard sale, estate sale, neighborhood sale, or other legitimate treasure-hunting opportunity that isn't on the map, let Chica know.

🐾 Chica's Treasure-Hunting Tips

Start earlyThe best stuff moves fast.
Bring cashWhen appropriate — many sales prefer it.
Measuring tapeCheck dimensions before buying furniture.
Reusable bagsCarry more, leave less mess.
Respect hoursDon't show up before the posted start.
Be politeYou're in someone's driveway.
Don't block drivewaysNeighbors still live there.
Verify firstListings change — check the source.
Bring a friendMore eyes, more fun.
Have funThat's the whole point.

Chica's Golden Rule: If someone is selling it from their driveway, they're still your neighbor. Be kind.
