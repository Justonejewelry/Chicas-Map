Meet Cheeks

Also answers to Chica, "Who's a good girl?", and the sound of a cheese wrapper

Hi! I'm Chica. 👋

If you're family, friends, or one of my loyal fans, you probably know me as Cheeks. Nobody remembers how it started… but somehow it stuck. I answer to Chica, Cheeks, "Who's a good girl?", and the unmistakable sound of a cheese wrapper.

I'm a tiny brown Chihuahua with a frosted little face, white socks, and a white-tipped tail that operates at maximum wag whenever snacks are nearby.

Here's what you should know:

  • 🐿️ Backyard Security Chief — Squirrels are under constant surveillance.
  • 🦴 Professional Napper — I can sleep through a thunderstorm… unless someone opens the treat jar.
  • 🦸 Part-Time Superhero — I have a cape. That's all the proof you need.
  • 🗺️ Chief Sniffing Officer — My human built the garage sale map. I inspect it for quality control… and hidden snack locations.

People say my human runs this place.
That's adorable.
I simply allow him to think that.
