Meet Chica

Official Chica only — identical likeness, no substitutes

Hi. I'm Chica.

They call me Meet Chica — and if you've seen the smile, you know why. I'm a brown Chihuahua with a little grey on my face, white on my feet, and a tip of white on my tail. I nap hard (tongue out is standard equipment), I rock a sparkly cape when the mission calls for it, and I go on adventures with my human.

This backyard is mine. The map? I help run that too. When I'm not supervising garage-sale intel, I'm either mid-power-nap on a purple blanket or showing off the famous Meet Chica grin.

Welcome to my corner. Wipe your paws.

— Chica
