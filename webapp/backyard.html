The story so far

Nobody remembers how "Cheeks" started… but somehow it stuck. I answer to Chica, Cheeks, "who's a good girl?", and the unmistakable sound of a cheese wrapper.

I'm a tiny brown Chihuahua with a frosted little face, white socks, and a white-tipped tail that operates at maximum wag whenever snacks are nearby.

  • 🐿️Backyard Security Chief — squirrels are under constant surveillance.
  • 🦴Professional Napper — I can sleep through a thunderstorm… unless someone opens the treat jar.
  • 🦸Part-Time Superhero — I have a cape. That's all the proof you need.
  • 🗺️Chief Sniffing Officer — my human built the garage sale map. I inspect it for quality control… and hidden snack locations.

People say my human runs this place. That's adorable. I simply allow him to think that.
