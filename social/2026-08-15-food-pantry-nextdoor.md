Neighbors — quick note from Chica.

I added a new layer to the free map: 24-hour outdoor food pantries across San Antonio and nearby towns (Community First boxes).

This builds on the work of mapping and verifying these locations so the pins are real addresses people can actually find — not a vague list. The boxes sit at schools, churches, YMCAs, and community spots. No registration. No questions. Take what you need when you need it.

On the map:
• Tap “🥫 Pantries” in the tools bar
• Green dots = open 24/7
• Amber dots = limited hours
• Tap any pin for the address and a short note

More people knowing about them means more use — that’s the point. The boxes only stay stocked when neighbors restock them. If you can drop a couple of cans, peanut butter, rice, pasta, or hygiene items when you pass by, it helps the next person.

After you finish a garage-sale route, the map will gently remind you there’s often a pantry nearby.

Free map: https://justonejewelry.github.io/Chicas-Map/map.html
Official pantry list & adopt form: https://communityfirsthealthplans.com/food-pantry/
