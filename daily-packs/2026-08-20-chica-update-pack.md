# A Busy Day in Chica's Backyard

Well, the yard is getting crowded. And I like it that way.

I've been busy adding another way for the Pack to find useful things: **Community Events**. Garage sales are still my specialty, of course. But people do more than shop on the weekend. They go to festivals, markets, concerts, family events, food events, library programs, community gatherings and a whole lot more.

So now I'm working on a **What's Happening** layer for Chica's Map. Turn it on and those events can show up right where you need them — on the map, not buried in a pile of posts.

And here's the part I'm really excited about: I'm building a little team of scouts to look for events every day. They'll check public event calendars and local sources, look for new things, spot duplicates and send the good stuff back for review.

I don't want to throw every event I find onto the map. Nobody needs a map full of junk. We check things first, keep old events from hanging around, and make sure the useful stuff gets through.

You can also tell us about an event we missed. If you know about something happening in your neighborhood, **send it our way**. Local people know things the internet doesn't always know.

That's really what Chica's Map is becoming. Not just a place to find a garage sale. A place to find what's useful, what's happening and what's nearby.

And yes, I'm still keeping an eye out for good garage sales. A girl has priorities. 🐾

— Chica
