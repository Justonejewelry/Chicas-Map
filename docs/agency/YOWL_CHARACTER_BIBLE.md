## Non-negotiable appearance
- Brown/tan short coat with cream chest, muzzle, and brow blaze
- Oversized dark brown eyes, soft highlight, friendly expression
- Large upright ears with pink inner ear
- **Black studded harness** with silver cone studs + dual chest buckles (always on)
- Soft closed-mouth smile unless dialogue requires open mouth
- Same proportions from any angle — do not redesign the face or harness

## Allowed
- Hundreds of wardrobe layers **over** the harness (hats, bandanas, jackets) — harness stays visible
- Any camera angle (3/4, profile, over-shoulder, map-pointing)
- Expressions: smile, wink, excited, pointing, talking mouth shapes

## Forbidden
- Different dog breed or longer snout
- Removing or redesigning the studded harness
- Dark gritty redesign; Yowl stays bright and approachable
