## 1. Simple Instructions (do this every session)

### Before you dial (5 minutes)
1. Open the live map + this sheet.
2. Pick **5 targets** from the Priority List (same category = same script muscle).
3. Fill the **one-liner** for each: *why them, this weekend*.
4. Have tiers ready: **Pin · Featured · Weekend Title** (prices below).
5. Timer on. **Block = 45–60 min.** No email-first unless they demand it.

### During the call
- Stand up. Smile on the open.
- **Goal of call 1:** Decision-maker + next step on calendar. Not a full close every time.
- Track on the sheet: date · contact · result · follow-up date.

### After the block
- Log results same day.
- Same-day text/email only if they asked for it.
- Follow-ups: **48 hours**, then **Tuesday before weekend**.

### Pricing anchors (adjust once; stay consistent)
| Tier | What they get | Suggested ask |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **Map Pin** | Logo pin on map + link | $75–150 / mo |
| **Featured Partner** | Pin + sidebar card + 1 social mention | $200–350 / mo |
| **Weekend Title** | Pin + “Presented by” on weekend forecast + Chica post | $400–600 / weekend |
| **Season** | 3 months Featured | 10% off prepaid |

Start higher; trade value before you cut price.

## 3. High-Impact Script (Phone Ninja)

### Gatekeeper (10 seconds)
> “Hey — quick one. Who handles **local partnerships or marketing** for the [West side / San Antonio] location? I only need a name.”

If pushed:  
> “It’s about **weekend map traffic** for people already out doing garage sales — not a cold pitch deck. Thirty seconds with the right person.”

### Decision-maker open (pattern interrupt)
> “Hi [Name] — [Your name] with **YardBird**, the garage-sale map for San Antonio.  
> Not calling to sell you a banner nobody clicks.  
> Calling because **thousands of SA treasure hunters** open our map Thu–Sat looking for sales — and the businesses next to those routes print money.  
> Got 45 seconds?”

*(Pause. Let them say yes.)*

### 30-second core pitch
> “YardBird is a live map of **verified garage, yard, and estate sales** across San Antonio.  
> Shoppers open it to plan their Saturday loop.  
> We’re placing a limited number of **partner pins** — U-Haul, storage, estate companies, flea markets — right where those routes run.  
> You’re not buying ‘awareness.’ You’re buying **presence in the moment someone decides where to drive and what to haul.**  
> We have a **Featured** slot open for [their category] this month.  
> Want me to hold it while I send a one-page visual, or do you want numbers right now?”

### Assumptive micro-close options
1. **Soft:** “I’ll send the one-pager + map screenshot — what’s the best email, and are you free Thursday for a 10-minute look?”
2. **Hard:** “Pin is $___ / month or Featured at $___ — do you want to start with this weekend’s Title slot or the monthly pin?”
3. **Binary:** “Better fit — **map pin near the Northwest clusters**, or **weekend title on the forecast panel**?”

### Voicemail (max 20 seconds)
> “[Name], [Your name] with YardBird — the San Antonio garage-sale map.  
> Partner pins for [moving / estate / flea] are limited this month.  
> I’ll try you again [day]. Or grab me at [phone] / mr.jsciaraffa@gmail.com.  
> It’s about weekend traffic, not another banner.”

## 4. Objection Rebuttals (short · calm · pivot)

**“Just email me.”**  
> “I will — and I’ll keep it to one page. So I send the right version: are you more **truck/storage**, or **estate/weekend events**? And is [email] still best?”

**“We don’t do sponsorships.”**  
> “Totally fair. This isn’t a radio buy. It’s a **map pin on the route your customers already drive on sale weekends**. Most partners treat it like a **location listing**, not a brand campaign. Worth a 10-minute look?”

**“Too expensive.”**  
> “Compared to what — a weekend of Facebook boosts or a truck wrap?  
> The pin is built so **one extra rental or one extra estate walk-in** covers the month.  
> We can start with a **single weekend Title** so you see the traffic before a monthly.”

**“Send a proposal / deck.”**  
> “I don’t do 12-page decks. One page: map screenshot, pin placement, price, start date.  
> I’ll send it in the next hour — can we book **10 minutes Thursday** so it doesn’t die in the inbox?”

**“We already advertise on Craigslist / Facebook.”**  
> “Perfect — those are **listings**. We’re the **map people use after they decide to go out**. Different job.  
> Partners use us so they’re **visible in the loop**, not lost in the feed.”

**“Not interested.”**  
> “Appreciate the straight answer. If the **[category] exclusive** opens up later this season, OK to check back once?”  
> *(Log. Move on. No chase.)*

**“Who else is on it?”**  
> “We’re keeping categories tight on purpose — one featured moving partner, one estate, one market — so you’re not stacked on top of three competitors. That’s the value.”

**“How many users / impressions?”**  
> “We’re honest: early growth stage, San Antonio first, live map with real verified sales.  
> You’re buying **intent density** (people actively routing to sales), not vanity impressions.  
> First partners lock **category position** before the graph curves up.”

**“Call me next quarter.”**  
> “Can do. Garage-sale season peaks spring and fall — locking a **pilot weekend** now is cheaper than fighting for space later.  
> Want me to pencil a **September weekend** so it’s on the calendar?”

**“I need to talk to my partner / corporate.”**  
> “Smart. What do they care about most — **cost**, **exclusivity**, or **proof**?  
> I’ll send the one-pager aimed at that, and you tell me the best time for a three-way five-minute call.”
